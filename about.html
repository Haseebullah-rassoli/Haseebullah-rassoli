ABOUT THE PROJECT

About & Contact

An independent educational project focused on practical resources and transparent, evidence-based guidance.

Our Story & Mission

Haseebullah Rassoli is an author and independent computer science and cybersecurity learner building practical digital resources. The hub brings together cyber-safety education, carefully reviewed public opportunities, original books, resume tools and digital services.

This is an independent project, not an official government, university or law-enforcement service, nor a claim of professional cybersecurity certification.

Verification Method

We check the original institution or organization, review the actual wording of its announcement, compare URLs and public contacts, note the date and identify information that remains unresolved. A verified source supports a specific fact; it does not establish that every message or offer using that name is safe.

Evidence labels: Confirmed specific fact, Needs caution, and Unconfirmed. Where evidence is incomplete, we share practical guidance instead of an unsupported allegation or 100% safety guarantee.

Corrections & Privacy

Send the relevant page URL and supporting material if you notice an error or outdated fact. Important corrections will be added to the website before social summaries are updated. Public cases must remove submitter identifiers and unnecessary private information.

The website is static and does not operate its own submissions database. When activated, review and business submissions use the Google-hosted Apps Script form and the owner’s private Google Sheet; optional redacted screenshots are saved to a private Google Drive folder. The form does not require opening your email application. The site owner controls access and retention of stored submissions. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment credentials or unredacted identity documents.
