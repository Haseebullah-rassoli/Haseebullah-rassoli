About & Contact

An independent learning and publishing project by Haseebullah Rassoli, creating practical digital resources and evidence-based public guidance.

Mission & Story

The hub combines cybersecurity awareness, carefully reviewed opportunities, books and useful tools. It is an independent project and does not claim official endorsement or professional security certification.

How We Verify

We begin with the original organization, institution or platform. We compare the claim with primary sources, record what was actually stated, check the date and explain material uncertainty. “Confirmed” refers only to specific evidence-backed facts, not a blanket guarantee that an organization, link, product or opportunity is safe.

When evidence is insufficient, we provide practical caution and say what remains unconfirmed. User-submitted experiences are claims until independently checked.

Corrections Policy

If a page is inaccurate or has become outdated, send its link and the supporting official source. We review corrections and update the master website article first. Material changes should be clearly noted. Paid clients do not receive favorable findings in exchange for payment.

Privacy & Submissions

This static website does not run a user database. The review form prepares an email in your email application; it does not upload text to GitHub Pages. If you send an email, its contents are handled by the email providers involved. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full card numbers, identity documents or unnecessary personal information. We do not publish identifiable community submissions by default. Contact us to request correction or deletion of material you submitted.

Feedback & Suggestions
