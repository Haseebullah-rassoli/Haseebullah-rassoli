Phishing & Fake Emails

Inspect the sender's full address, avoid opening unexpected attachments, and do not follow a message's link to verify the message itself. Visit the organization's known official site independently.

Fake Jobs & Interviews

Confirm that the job is listed on the employer's official recruitment website. Ask whether the named recruiter works there using independently verified contact details. Do not pay interview or placement fees based only on a message.

Account & Password Safety

Use unique passwords, multi-factor authentication where available, and keep devices and applications updated. Never share one-time verification codes.

Finding Official Sources

Compare the original domain, announcement details, dates, eligibility conditions and contact information. Record what was checked and what is still unknown.

Case Reviews & Evidence Labels

Confirmed fact: directly evidenced. Needs caution: material warning signs. Unconfirmed: insufficient evidence. We do not publish raw private correspondence or imply certainty where it is unavailable.

Need urgent help? If money or an account may be compromised, contact your bank, email provider or relevant platform directly through its official channel. This community review is not an emergency service.
