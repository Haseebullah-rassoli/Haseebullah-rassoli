Our evidence labels

Confirmed from an official source: specific facts supported by a cited primary source. Needs caution: warning signs or conflicting details. Unconfirmed: not enough evidence to determine the claim. An official-looking page is not, by itself, proof that an offer or message is safe.

If a case cannot be verified, we publish the observable facts and practical safety guidance rather than an unsupported accusation or a promise of safety.
