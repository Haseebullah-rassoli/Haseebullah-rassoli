Community safety

Submit for Review

Seen a suspicious email, job offer, scholarship announcement or link? Share the claim for possible manual assessment. We publish guidance only after examining available evidence.

Before sharing

Never include passwords, one-time codes, full payment-card numbers, CVV, passport or ID numbers, private addresses or unnecessary personal details. Remove these from screenshots before sending. Do not click or open suspicious links just to collect evidence.

Submissions are not published automatically. A submission does not guarantee a reply, investigation or definitive verdict. Urgent account compromise should be reported to the relevant service or bank directly.

What to include

Briefly describe the claim, where you saw it, the organization it claims to represent, any request for money, and the text of the suspicious URL if already available. You may send a redacted screenshot by email.

We may publish an anonymized educational case only after review. We do not disclose your identity or personal submission by default.
