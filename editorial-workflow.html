Transparent editorial process

How a Report Becomes Public Guidance

Submission is not publication. We review evidence, remove personal data, describe uncertainty and make corrections when needed.

1. Receive

People send a redacted description of a suspicious email, website, scholarship, job or advertisement through their email app. No attachments are automatically uploaded to this site. We never request passwords, one-time codes, full payment details or identity documents.

2. Triage

We identify the claim, relevant date, alleged sender, link text and potential harm. Do not open dangerous attachments or visit suspicious links just to collect evidence. Urgent account or bank compromise must be reported directly to the provider.

3. Cross-check

Look for an original official announcement, a verified organizational contact route, exact eligibility and funding terms, domain discrepancies, and independent corroboration where available. Record the source URLs and the date checked. Absence of a search result alone does not prove fraud.

4. Assess the evidence

Confirmed specific fact: supported by an attributable primary source. Needs caution: warning signs or contradictory evidence. Unconfirmed: not enough information to establish the claim. Never promise 100% safety or call a person or organization fraudulent solely on suspicion.

5. Protect the submitter

Redact names, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, document numbers, tracking identifiers and identifiable screenshots. Seek explicit permission before quoting or identifying a contributor. Do not publish raw emails or sensitive documents in the public repository.

6. Publish and correct

Publish an evidence-based summary, source links, date checked, practical next steps and outstanding questions. Social summaries link back to the website. Corrections update the website first and note material changes. Paid reviews and sponsored posts are clearly identified and do not change the evidence standard.

Scope: This independent educational project is not a law-enforcement agency, certified forensic laboratory or emergency response service. Review requests are handled as capacity allows.
