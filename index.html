How to submit safely

Evidence before labels

Confirmed: specific facts directly supported by reliable primary evidence.

Needs caution: credible warning signs or unresolved conflicts.

Unconfirmed: insufficient evidence to judge the claim.

We do not guarantee that a link, organization or offer is 100% safe. Uncertain cases receive clear guidance, not unsupported accusations.
