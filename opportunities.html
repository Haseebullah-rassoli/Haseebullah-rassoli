Before you apply: check these details

Confirm the organizer and use its official application page.

  1. Eligibility: nationality, age, education and required experience.
  2. Costs: application fees, tuition, travel, accommodation and what funding actually covers.
  3. Requirements: language tests, documents, references and attendance mode.
  4. Timing: closing date, time zone, interview dates and program start.
  5. Travel: confirm visa requirements with the relevant official authority.
  6. Evidence: save the original announcement and the date you checked it. Ask the organizer about missing details.

These are checking steps, not a confirmation that you qualify for any particular program.
