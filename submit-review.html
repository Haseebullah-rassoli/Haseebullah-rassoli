COMMUNITY SAFETY

Submit for Review

Send a suspicious claim for possible manual review. We assess available evidence and provide guidance; we do not guarantee a response or definitive verdict.

Before you submit

Remove personal details from any description or screenshot. Never share a password, one-time code, card number, CVV, passport number or other sensitive document.

Describe what the message claims, the organization it names, any request for payment and the warning signs you noticed. You can include a URL as plain text if you already have it; do not click suspicious links just to investigate.

Privacy first: This form uses FormSubmit, a third-party service. Your email application will not open, but a short browser verification step may appear. Please send redacted details only.

What happens next?

We may review the evidence, seek an official source and prepare anonymized guidance. A submission is not published automatically, and no reply or conclusion is guaranteed.

Send a Redacted Report

A third-party service processes submissions. Include only information you are comfortable sharing.

The site owner must complete one-time email activation for delivery. There is no file-upload feature yet.
