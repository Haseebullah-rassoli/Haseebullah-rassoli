Submit for Review

Concerned about an email, job offer, scholarship message or website? Share a redacted report for possible evidence-based review. A submission does not imply a scam finding or safety guarantee.

Redacted reportsOptional screenshotManual review

HOW IT WORKS

Share the facts, not your private data.

This form is for educational review, not emergency incident response. Only send information you are comfortable sharing with the Google-hosted form and the owner’s private Google storage.

01

Describe the claim

Tell us what the sender or website said, what they asked you to do, and why you are concerned.

02

Remove personal details

Blur or crop names, addresses, contact details and identifiers before attaching a screenshot. Never include passwords, one-time codes, bank or card details, passport scans or private documents.

03

We check available evidence

We may compare the claim with official announcements and independently obtained contacts. Possible outcomes include confirmed specific facts, warning signs or insufficient evidence. No response or verdict is guaranteed.

Privacy & delivery

When activated, reports are saved in the owner’s private Google Sheet and redacted screenshots in a private Google Drive folder. The custom form is served by Google Apps Script; it is not a GitHub database. No report is published automatically. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or identity documents.
