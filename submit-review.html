HOW IT WORKS

Share the facts, not your private data.

This form is for educational review, not emergency incident response. Only send information you are comfortable sharing with the external form and email providers.

01

Describe the claim

Tell us what the sender or website said, what they asked you to do, and why you are concerned.

02

Remove personal details

Blur or crop names, addresses, contact details and identifiers before attaching a screenshot. Never include passwords, one-time codes, bank or card details, passport scans or private documents.

03

We check available evidence

We may compare the claim with official announcements and independently obtained contacts. Possible outcomes include confirmed specific facts, warning signs or insufficient evidence. No response or verdict is guaranteed.

Privacy & delivery

Your report goes through the third-party FormSubmit service and email providers, not a GitHub database. The website owner must complete one-time email activation before delivery can be relied on. Reports are not published automatically. FormSubmit states that it retains text submissions for up to 30 days; uploaded files are not retained in its submissions archive.
