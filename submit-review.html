COMMUNITY SAFETY • PRIVATE SUBMISSION

Submit for Review

Concerned about a fake link, phishing message, job offer, scholarship message or website? Share a redacted report for possible evidence-based review. A submission does not imply a scam finding or safety guarantee.

Redacted reportsOptional screenshotManual review

HOW IT WORKS

Share the facts, not your private data.

This form is for educational review, not emergency incident response. WhatsApp Business is the primary contact route. A separate Google-hosted form is an optional alternative; availability is controlled by its owner. Share only redacted information.

01

Describe the claim

Tell us what the sender or website said, what they asked you to do, and why you are concerned.

02

Remove personal details

Blur or crop names, addresses, contact details and identifiers before attaching a screenshot. Never include passwords, one-time codes, bank or card details, passport scans or private documents.

03

We check available evidence

We may compare the claim with official announcements and independently obtained contacts. Possible outcomes include confirmed specific facts, warning signs or insufficient evidence. No response or verdict is guaranteed.

Privacy & delivery

WhatsApp reports are handled in the owner's WhatsApp Business chat and are not automatically copied into Google Sheets. The optional Google form may store reports and redacted images in the owner’s Google Sheet and Drive. This website cannot confirm its storage or delivery status. No report is published automatically. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or identity documents.
